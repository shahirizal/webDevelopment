My Favorite Places

1. Sepang International Circuit

Sepang International Circuit is my favorite place because of my interest in cars, motorsport, and Formula 1. I would love to visit the circuit during a race weekend and experience the atmosphere in person.

2. Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur is one of my favorite places because of its modern city environment, interesting architecture, and variety of things to explore. I also enjoy taking photographs of cars and the city whenever I get the chance.

3. Putrajaya

I like Putrajaya because of its peaceful environment, beautiful architecture, and wide roads. It is also a nice place to explore and take photographs.

4. Genting Highlands

Genting Highlands is a place I would enjoy visiting because of its cool weather and mountain scenery. I also like the winding roads leading up to the highlands.

5. Kuantan, Pahang

Kuantan is a meaningful place to me because it is the capital city of my home state, Pahang. I enjoy its relaxed atmosphere, local surroundings, and places such as the Kuantan 188 tower and the city centre.

6. Taman Tasik Taiping, Perak

Taman Tasik Taiping is one of the places I would enjoy visiting because of its peaceful environment and beautiful greenery. The large rain trees and quiet roads make it a relaxing place to walk around and take photographs.
